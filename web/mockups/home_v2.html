How it works

Three checks before a single word is written

The safety check runs first, the guidelines are found second, and the writing comes last — only from what was found. If nothing is found, PediBot says so.

1 · safety

Warning signs first

A rule-based check looks for the signs emergency paediatricians publish for parents — a baby under 3 months with fever, vomiting after a head bump, a swallowed battery. If one matches, the answer opens with "go now".

2 · sources

Only published guidelines

49 documents from paediatric societies and public health bodies. No forums, no blogs. Each one is listed below with its organisation and a link to the original.

3 · writing

Every sentence names its source

"According to the SEUP…", "The WHO recommends…". A verifier rejects any reply that cites something that wasn't found, or states a dose the guideline didn't. Doses come from the calculator, never from the AI.

Dose calculator

Paracetamol and ibuprofen, by weight — in ml for your bottle

Type the name on your bottle (Calpol, Tylenol, Apiretal, Dalsy, Nurofen, Advil…) and your child's weight. Fixed tables from the AEPap dosing guide, with hard maximums. No AI involved.

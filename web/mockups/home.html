How it answers

Three checks before a single word is written

This order is the product. The safety check runs first, the sources are found second, and the writing comes last — and only from what was found.

1 / triage

Warning signs go to the top

Before anything else, a rule-based check looks for the warning signs paediatric emergency doctors publish for parents: breathing difficulty, a baby under 3 months with fever, vomiting after a head bump, a swallowed battery…

🚨 If one matches, the answer opens with "go to the emergency department now" — with your country's number.
2 / sources

Only published guidelines

Your question is matched against 49 documents from paediatric societies and public health bodies — no forums, no blogs, no "the internet". If nothing relevant is found, PediBot says so instead of guessing.

"I don't have reliable information on this in my sources" is a real answer here.
3 / writing

Every sentence points to a page

The reply is written in plain language from those passages only. Each clinical statement carries a reference, and a verifier rejects any reply that cites a source that wasn't found — or states a dose the guideline didn't.

Doses never come from the AI. They come from the calculator below, which uses fixed tables.

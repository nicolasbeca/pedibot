# Code of conduct

PediBot adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**
for everyone who takes part in the project: in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other
space the project runs.

## In short

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's background, language or experience. Many
  people this project is for do not write in English; neither do some of its contributors.
- Criticise ideas and code, never people. Accept feedback and give it constructively.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, publishing others' private
  information, or any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.
- This is a child-health project. Never post a real child's personal or medical details in an
  issue or pull request; describe cases in general terms.

## Reporting

Report anything that breaks this code to **pedibot.ai@gmail.com**. Reports are read by the
maintainer, handled confidentially, and answered. The maintainer may remove comments, commits
or contributions that break it, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone who does, as set
out in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.

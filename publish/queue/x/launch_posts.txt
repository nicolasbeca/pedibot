pedibot.xyz

=== 2 · How it's different ===
Most health chatbots answer from memory. PediBot doesn't.

Every question goes through 3 checks:
1. Warning signs first (no AI, just the emergency doctors' list)
2. Only published guidelines — 211 documents, nothing else
3. A verifier rejects any answer that cites something it didn't find

pedibot.xyz

=== 3 · Dose calculator ===
"How much Calpol for 14 kg?"

PediBot's dose calculator gives paracetamol and ibuprofen doses by weight, in ml for your actual bottle — Calpol, Tylenol, Apiretal, Dalsy, Nurofen, Advil, Motrin, Doliprane…

No AI involved. Fixed tables from the AEPap dosing guide, with hard maximums.

PediBot cannot say anything it can't point to.

211 sources, all public and listed with their licence: SEUP, AEP, NHS, CDC, MedlinePlus, WHO, Spanish Ministry of Health.

=== 8 · Oral rehydration ===
Diarrhoea or vomiting: how much rehydration solution, and how?

PediBot quotes the official leaflet: ~200 ml per loose stool for children over 1 year, in 25–30 ml sips every 10–15 min; after a vomit, 5–10 ml every 10 minutes.
